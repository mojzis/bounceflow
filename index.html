⚡ The Physics

The Elasticity bar shows the ball's current bounciness:

  • ● Red/Orange = Low bounce (ball won't bounce high)
  • ● Cyan/Green = High bounce (ball bounces really high)

In later levels, the ball's properties change over time - watch the color!

🧪 Materials & Weight (World 3)

  • Trampoline - super springy, and every hit makes the ball bouncier
  • Sponge - soaks up the bounce and makes the ball softer
  • Glass - only a heavy ball can smash through
  • Color gates - cyan gates let only bouncy balls through, red gates only soft ones
  • Power stars - stars with a ring change the ball: bouncier, softer, heavier or lighter
  • Fans - blow light balls far, but barely move heavy ones

Heavy and light balls fall at the same speed - just like in real life!

💡 Tips & Hints

  • Surfaces are thicker than they look - collision detection is generous
  • You can adjust surfaces while the ball is bouncing
  • Try different angles - there are multiple solutions!
  • Stuck? Press ? or click "Show Hint" to run the experimental solver
  • Watch as the solver tries different configurations (faint red lines)
  • When found, the solution appears as cyan dashed surfaces with angles
  • The solver adapts to random star positions - every hint is custom!
